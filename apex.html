Reach the summit

APEX Logo Branding

Brand identity for a computer hardware and technology brand.

Case study

Built to climb.

APEX makes computer hardware for people who push their machines to the limit: graphics cards, processors, memory and gaming gear. The brand needed a mark that feels fast, precise and engineered, and that stays sharp at any size, from a retail box down to a keycap.

The answer turns the letter A into a mountain peak. Three simple parts form one symbol, and the same angle, colours and shapes carry through the wordmark, the graphics and every application.

01 — The idea

Reach the summit.

Three parts, one climb. Each piece of the symbol has its own colour and its own job.

02 — Construction

One angle. One grid.

The symbol is drawn on a square grid, and every diagonal follows the same slope, from the summit down to the last circuit notch.

0Unit square. The symbol fits one square, with the summit on the centre axis.
2:1One angle. Every diagonal rises two units for each one across: 63.4° from the baseline.
0Three parts. Cap, circuit leg and blade leg, each in its own colour.
