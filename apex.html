Reach the summit

APEX Logo Branding

Brand identity for a computer hardware and technology brand.

Case study

Built to climb.

APEX makes computer hardware for people who push their machines to the limit: graphics cards, processors, memory and gaming gear. The brand needed a mark that feels fast, precise and engineered, and that stays sharp at any size, from a retail box down to a keycap.

The answer turns the letter A into a mountain peak. Three simple parts form one symbol, and the same angle, colours and shapes carry through the wordmark, the graphics and every application.

02 — Construction

One angle. One grid.

The symbol is drawn on a 172 unit square. Every diagonal, from the summit to the last circuit notch, follows the same 2 : 1 slope.

172Unit squareThe symbol fits one square. The summit sits on the centre axis.
2 : 1One angleEvery diagonal rises two units for each one across: 63.4° from the baseline.
3Three partsCap, circuit leg and blade leg, each in its own colour.
03 — Wordmark

Cut, slanted, sharp.

Custom letterforms built from the same geometry as the symbol.

The A

Built from the symbol, so name and mark share one slope.

The corners

P and E lose their sharp corners to 45° cuts, like a chip package.

The X

One blue slash cuts through the letter, the single colour accent in the name.

04 — Logo system

Pick the one that fits.

Three signatures from one set of shapes: a stacked primary logo, the icon on its own, and a horizontal logo for headers and wide spaces.

05 — Variations

One logo, six grounds.

Full colour needs a dark ground. Everywhere else the logo drops to a single colour.

06 — Colour

Five colours. One red.

Blue leads, graphite grounds it, silver carries the metal, and red is saved for the summit. Click a colour to copy its code.
